## One-line pitch

A camera running a PipeGen TensorRT pipeline spots an endangered animal at the road edge. Within milliseconds, a FirmGen-built ESP32 roadside unit checks the detection against its own sensors, picks a species-specific response, and flashes a warning to drivers. It then reports back exactly how long that took.

## The problem

Wildlife-vehicle collisions on Brazilian highways are a major cause of death for medium and large mammals. The five species in the BRA dataset are among the most frequently hit: lowland tapir and giant anteater (both IUCN *Vulnerable*), maned wolf (*Near Threatened*), puma and jaguarundi. A collision with a 250 kg tapir is also dangerous for the driver. Static "animal crossing" signs are always on, so drivers learn to ignore them. Drivers get no warning at the moment an animal is actually there.

## Our solution

WildGuard is a **closed perception-to-action loop**. The roadside sign turns on only when an animal is really at the road. How strongly it reacts depends on the species and on whether two independent sensors agree.

```
USB camera → PipeGen (YOLOv8n · TensorRT · tracker · road-zone ROI) → MQTT
           → bridge (3-frame confirmation · per-class thresholds · cool-down)
           → ESP32 / FirmGen (validate · sensor fusion · species profile · LEDs + buzzer ≤ 100 ms) → ACK → dashboard
```

1. **See (PipeGen, Track A).** A USB camera feeds a PipeGen v0.5.1 pipeline built through all five stages: Requirement, Pipeline IR, Model, Compiler and Run. Our custom YOLOv8n is compiled to a TensorRT [[FP16]] engine and runs in DeepStream with a multi-object tracker and a road-zone ROI. It publishes per-object JSON over MQTT, plus JSON metadata and an annotated RTSP stream.
2. **Confirm (bridge).** A small gateway turns PipeGen's per-frame stream into one confirmed event per animal. It keeps the 5 target classes, applies per-class confidence thresholds chosen from validation data, requires 3 consecutive frames per track, checks the road zone and applies a 5 s cool-down per track. Each event is under 256 bytes.
3. **Decide and act (FirmGen, Track B).** The ESP32 runs ESP-IDF v5.5 firmware generated by FirmGen from our PRD, with [[30]] frozen requirements. It reads its own HC-SR04 ultrasonic and PIR sensors, validates and deduplicates each event, and maps the species to a response profile. It **fuses** the camera event with its own sensor state: agreement raises severity by one level, and camera-only lowers it by one. It then drives a strobing warning sign and buzzer within 100 ms of receipt and publishes an ACK with on-board timestamps.
4. **Prove it (mission control).** A React dashboard shows the loop live: pipeline FPS, broker, ESP32 state, live sensor values, board telemetry, and every alert with its **measured** capture-to-ACK latency. It also offers a kill switch, synthetic test alerts and a one-click evidence pack.

### Species response, decided on the ESP32

| Species | IUCN | Camera only | Camera + board sensor | Roadside sign / buzzer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tapir | Vulnerable | HIGH | CRITICAL | Fast red strobe + amber / 3 long beeps, repeating |
| Giant anteater | Vulnerable | MEDIUM | CRITICAL | Red strobe 4 Hz / 2 beeps |
| Maned wolf | Near Threatened | MEDIUM | CRITICAL | Red/amber alternate / 2 short beeps |
| Puma | Least Concern | LOW | HIGH | Slow red pulse / 1 beep |
| Jaguarundi | Least Concern | WARN | MEDIUM | Amber blink / silent |

## How PipeGen and FirmGen connect (Closed Loop)

The connection is a versioned **MQTT contract**. It is written into the FirmGen PRD as requirements, so it shows up in the traceability report:

| Requirement | What it guarantees |
|---|---|
| WG-CON-03 | The board subscribes to `wildguard/perception/detections` on every reconnect |
| WG-EVT-01..05 | Validate, threshold, deduplicate, map species, fuse with the board's own sensor |
| WG-ACT-03 | Actuator on within 100 ms of message receipt |
| WG-ACK-01 | Exactly one ACK per event, carrying the on-board receive and actuate timestamps |

Each tool is load-bearing. **Remove PipeGen** and nothing sees: there is no other inference path in the live system. **Remove FirmGen** and nothing acts: species logic, sensor fusion, fail-safe and the ACK all live on the board. The loop also degrades safely. If the laptop link drops, the board enters DEGRADED mode and keeps a sensor-only amber warning running.

## Limitations and next steps

- **Closed-set model:** it has no person, dog or background class, so look-alikes can be misclassified. Mitigated by 3-frame confirmation, per-class thresholds, the road-zone ROI and on-board sensor fusion. Next: retrain with background images and more epochs (the baseline trained for only 10 epochs on CPU).
- **Tapir recall** at the alert threshold is 0.51 per frame. Tracking across many frames raises the chance of catching the animal, but more training data for this CRITICAL class is the first priority.
- **Bench-scale sensing:** the HC-SR04 has about 4 m range and stands in for radar, LiDAR or a PIR array on a real road.
- **Deployment:** solar power, LoRa for roads without Wi-Fi, multi-camera coverage, TLS on the broker, and a pole-mounted Jetson with an INT8 engine.
